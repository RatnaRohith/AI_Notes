Typical Expert System – DART
1. What is DART?

DART (Dynamic Analysis and Replanning Tool) is a well-known expert system/planning system developed for the U.S. military.

It was designed to help with logistics planning and transportation scheduling, particularly the movement of military personnel, equipment, and resources.

DART is an important example of how AI can be used for planning, scheduling, resource allocation, and decision support.

2. Purpose of DART

The main purpose of DART was to help military planners answer questions such as:

Which resources should be transported?
Where should they be transported?
Which transportation routes should be used?
How should personnel and equipment be scheduled?
How can transportation plans be changed when circumstances change?

In simple terms:

Military Requirements
        ↓
Available Resources
        ↓
Transportation Constraints
        ↓
DART
        ↓
Plan / Schedule
        ↓
Movement of Personnel & Equipment
3. Why is DART an Expert System?

DART uses domain knowledge, constraints, planning techniques, and reasoning to produce useful transportation plans.

Instead of simply storing information, the system reasons about:

Resources
Locations
Transportation capacity
Time
Routes
Constraints
Priorities
Changes in the situation

Therefore, it represents a practical application of AI-based planning and decision support.

4. Major Functions of DART
1. Logistics Planning

DART helps determine how military resources can be moved efficiently.

For example:

Equipment A → Location X
Personnel B → Location Y
Supplies C → Location Z

The system determines an appropriate transportation plan.

2. Scheduling

DART determines when different transportation activities should occur.

Vehicle 1 → 08:00
Vehicle 2 → 09:30
Aircraft 1 → 11:00

The schedule must satisfy resource and time constraints.

3. Resource Allocation

Available transportation resources are assigned to different requirements.

For example:

Available:
    10 trucks
    5 aircraft
    20 transport units

Requirements:
    Personnel
    Vehicles
    Supplies

DART determines how these resources should be allocated.

4. Constraint Handling

Real-world military logistics involves many constraints.

Examples:

Limited transportation capacity
Limited time
Route restrictions
Availability of vehicles
Personnel requirements
Priority of missions

DART attempts to produce plans that satisfy these constraints.

5. Dynamic Replanning

One of DART's important capabilities was its ability to modify plans when circumstances changed.

For example:

Original Plan
     ↓
Transportation route blocked
     ↓
Situation changes
     ↓
DART analyzes the problem
     ↓
New plan generated

This is why Dynamic Analysis and Replanning Tool is an appropriate description of the system.

7. Example

Suppose a military organization needs to move:

100 soldiers
20 vehicles
500 supply units

from Location A to Location B.

Available resources:

5 transport aircraft
10 trucks

The system must consider:

Number of personnel
Amount of equipment
Vehicle capacity
Travel time
Available transportation
Priority

It can then produce a transportation schedule.

If one aircraft becomes unavailable:

Original Plan
     ↓
Aircraft unavailable
     ↓
Recalculate resources
     ↓
Change assignments
     ↓
Generate revised plan

This illustrates dynamic replanning.

9. Advantages of DART
1. Handles complex planning

Military logistics can involve thousands of resources and constraints.

2. Saves planning time

Computer-assisted planning can significantly reduce the effort required from human planners.

3. Handles changing situations

Plans can be modified when resources or circumstances change.

4. Supports decision making

DART assists human planners rather than simply presenting raw data.

5. Practical AI application

It demonstrated that AI planning techniques could be applied to large-scale real-world logistics problems.

10. Limitations
1. Domain-specific

DART was designed primarily for military logistics and transportation.

2. Complex knowledge requirements

Accurate planning requires extensive information about resources, locations, transportation, and constraints.

3. Dependent on available information

Incorrect or outdated information can result in poor plans.

4. Human supervision

Military decisions still require human judgment and authorization.

12. Exam Answer

DART (Dynamic Analysis and Replanning Tool) is an AI-based planning and decision-support system developed for military logistics. It assists military planners in transportation planning, scheduling, and resource allocation. DART considers resources, locations, transportation capacities, priorities, and constraints to generate feasible plans. An important feature of DART is dynamic replanning, which allows plans to be modified when the situation or available resources change. It is a significant example of the application of AI to large-scale logistics and planning problems.
