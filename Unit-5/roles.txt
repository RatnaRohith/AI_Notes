Roles of Expert Systems

An Expert System is an AI system that uses a knowledge base and an inference engine to solve problems and make decisions in a specific domain, similar to a human expert.

Major Roles of Expert Systems
1. Decision Making

Expert systems help users make decisions by analyzing available facts and applying expert knowledge.

Example:
A medical expert system analyzes symptoms and suggests possible diseases.

2. Diagnosis

They identify the cause of a problem based on observed symptoms or conditions.

Examples:

Medical diagnosis
Machine fault diagnosis
Network failure diagnosis
Car fault diagnosis

Example rule:

IF engine does not start
AND battery voltage is low
THEN battery may be faulty.
3. Prediction and Forecasting

Expert systems can predict future conditions based on current information and historical knowledge.

Examples:

Weather prediction
Predicting machine failures
Financial risk assessment
Crop disease prediction
4. Planning

They help determine a sequence of actions required to achieve a particular goal.

Examples:

Production planning
Project planning
Robot task planning
Treatment planning
5. Design

Expert systems assist experts in designing systems, products, or configurations.

Examples:

Computer system configuration
Electronic circuit design
Building design
Network design
6. Monitoring

They continuously observe a system and identify abnormal conditions.

Example:

An industrial expert system monitors:

Temperature
Pressure
Voltage
Machine speed

If a value exceeds a safe limit, it can generate an alarm or warning.

7. Interpretation

Expert systems interpret complex data and convert it into meaningful information.

Examples:

Interpreting medical test results
Interpreting geological data
Analyzing sensor data
Interpreting satellite images
8. Troubleshooting

They help users find and correct problems in a system.

Example:

A computer troubleshooting system may reason:

IF computer does not power ON
AND power indicator is OFF
THEN check power supply.
9. Instruction and Training

Expert systems can act as intelligent tutors by explaining concepts and guiding users through problem-solving.

Examples:

Student tutoring systems
Medical training systems
Technical training systems

They can also explain why a particular conclusion was reached.

10. Recommendation

Expert systems recommend suitable actions, products, or solutions based on user requirements.

Examples:

Financial investment recommendations
Equipment selection
Medical treatment suggestions
Product configuration
11. Consultation

They provide expert-level advice to users who may not have access to a human specialist.

Example:

A farmer can enter information about:

Soil
Crop
Weather
Symptoms

The system can recommend possible treatments or actions.

12. Automation of Routine Expert Tasks

Expert systems can automate repetitive tasks that normally require specialized knowledge.

Examples:

Loan evaluation
Equipment inspection
Insurance assessment
Technical support

In one sentence

The main role of an expert system is to capture expert knowledge and use it to perform tasks such as diagnosis, decision-making, prediction, planning, monitoring, troubleshooting, recommendation, and consultation.
